# Gallery Mirrors proof of concept

20 real Gallery mirrors, each with a primary image and alternate images, have replaced the 12 illustrative mirror records. 106 images are held in the site asset structure (20 primary, 86 alternate). The original unsuffixed filename is always selected first; numbered variants retain numeric order. Image derivatives use a maximum 1800-pixel edge with unchanged aspect ratio. All selected source images are retained as gallery frames.

The workbook has 319 mirror records and unique barcodes/ImageKeys. The ZIP has 494 images, 234 keys, of which 169 keys match the workbook's Mirrors sheet. The Windsor 567929 gallery uses the eight earlier supplied matching images because that key is absent from this ZIP.

## Data limitations

- All workbook mirror rows have blank Shape, Colour, Width, Height and Style fields. Shape and colour are populated only from explicit name terms. Round diameters and equal two-axis dimensions provide reliable width/height values. Other dimensions remain visible in their original order without guessing axes.
- No reliable SKU column is supplied: ImageKey is used as the prototype product reference; the full barcode is retained.
- The Range field often contains “Gallery” rather than a specific coordinated collection; source values are retained as supplied.
- Material, style, orientation and mounting category remain blank when unsupported. Mirror category assignments use explicit leaner/overmantel/wall wording only.
- Availability reflects the workbook snapshot, not a live stock feed.
- Only RRP is included in the customer product data. Trade price stays behind the existing sign-in message. Stock quantities, costs, margins, MOQ and performance fields are excluded from the shipped product data.
- Navigation, filters, typography, spacing, card design and layouts are unchanged. Art, Clocks and the other department datasets are unchanged.
